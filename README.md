##  Ethics

Everything here is done on **legal practice targets only**: my own machines, deliberately vulnerable labs, CTFs, and platforms such as TryHackMe and PortSwigger. I do not test systems I don't own or have permission to test. I also don't publish flags or full solutions to active challenges, only the concepts I learned.
